- [mkdtemp](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/TemporaryDirectories.cpp) (libc) - six trailing `X` characters are replaced from host entropy and retried through the guest `mkdir` export with guest errno values, following the standard `mkdtemp` contract; the NID `Qn+tBdUwoBs` comes from the public symbol catalogue, and console behaviour on unmapped templates and collision exhaustion is not measured.
